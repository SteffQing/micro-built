# MicroBuilt Prime

MicroBuilt Prime gives salary-backed loans to salary earners in Nigeria, mostly public servants, whose pay MicroBuilt
can deduct from through their employer's payroll. Customers apply in the app, and repay through a fixed monthly
deduction from their salary, so there is nothing to remember to pay.

## Loans

- **Cash loans:** money paid into the customer's bank account. A customer picks what the loan is for (education,
  personal, business, medical, rent, travel, agriculture, utilities, emergency or other) and the amount.
- **Asset (commodity) loans:** the customer asks for an item from MicroBuilt's catalogue instead of cash. The team
  reviews the request and, when it is approved, sets the amount and the number of months; it is then repaid like a cash
  loan.
- What a loan costs is a monthly interest charge on the amount borrowed, spread evenly over the deductions, plus a
  one-time management fee taken from the money paid out. The rates are shown in the app before a customer confirms a
  request, and a running loan keeps the rates it was approved with.
- The team reviews every request. The customer is told in the app when it is approved, and again when the money is
  sent.

## Loan statuses

- **Pending:** waiting for the team to review it. A pending request can still be edited or withdrawn.
- **Approved:** accepted; the money hasn't been sent yet.
- **Disbursed:** the money has been paid out and the loan is running: deductions are being taken.
- **Repaid:** fully paid off.
- **Rejected:** not approved. The customer can apply again.

## How a month's deduction works

Each month MicroBuilt sends every employer a list of what to deduct from each borrower's salary (a "variation").
Payroll deducts it and sends back a record of what was actually deducted (a "voucher"). When that record comes in,
each borrower's deduction for the month is marked:

- **Expected / awaiting:** the month's deduction has been sent to payroll and MicroBuilt is waiting for the result.
- **Fulfilled:** the full amount was deducted.
- **Partial:** only part of it was deducted. A default charge is added on the part that wasn't covered.
- **Failed:** nothing was deducted that month. A default charge is added on the amount that wasn't covered.

Timing depends on the employer's payroll: a month's result usually shows a few days to a few weeks after payday. If a
customer thinks a deduction is wrong, the team can look into it.

## Top-ups

While a loan is running, a customer can ask to borrow more on top of it (a top-up). The team reviews it like a new
request; once it is paid out, the balance and the monthly deduction are worked out again.

## Paying off early (liquidation)

A customer can pay off part or all of their balance early. In the app they see how much clears the loan today, make the
payment to MicroBuilt's account, and upload the proof. The team checks the payment and then applies it. Paying early
lowers what is owed, so the deductions after it are smaller.

## Changing the number of months (tenure)

The number of months a loan runs for can be changed by the team, for example to make the monthly deduction smaller. A
change is proposed and then approved by the team; the customer is told when it takes effect.

## Statements

Customers can see every charge and payment on their loan, with a running balance, on the Statement page, and download
it as a PDF or spreadsheet. The file is prepared in the background and arrives as a notification with a download link.

## Accounts and security

- Customers sign up with an email address, a phone number or both, and sign in with a password, an emailed code, a
  magic link or a passkey.
- Two-factor authentication (an authenticator app) can be turned on in Settings → Security.
- Customers' details (profile, identity, bank account) are changed by sending a change request from Settings; the team
  approves it before it takes effect.
