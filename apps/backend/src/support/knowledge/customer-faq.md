# Customer help

## Applying for a loan

1. Sign up at the MicroBuilt Prime site and verify your email or phone number.
2. Add your details: your employer and payroll details, your identity details and the bank account the money should
   go to. The team confirms them before your first loan.
3. Open **Loans/Asset Request** (`/loan-request`) and choose **Cash loan** or **Asset loan**. For a cash loan, pick what
   it is for and the amount; for an asset loan, pick the item.
4. The team reviews it. You are told in the app when it is approved and when the money is sent.

**Requirements:** you need to be a salary earner whose employer's payroll MicroBuilt can deduct from, with a bank account
in your name and your identity details. What you can borrow depends on your salary and any loan you already have; your
account officer can confirm what you qualify for.

## Updating your details

Your profile, identity and bank account are changed through a change request, so nobody can change where your money
goes without the team checking it. Go to **Settings** (`/settings`), edit the details and submit. You'll see the request
as pending until the team approves or rejects it, and you're notified either way.

## What your loan status means

- **Pending:** the team hasn't reviewed it yet. You can still edit or withdraw it on the Loans page.
- **Approved:** it was accepted and the money is being prepared.
- **Disbursed:** the money was sent; your monthly deductions have started.
- **Repaid:** you've paid it off.
- **Rejected:** it wasn't approved. You can apply again.

## When a deduction failed or was partial

Payroll sometimes deducts less than expected, or nothing, for example when salary was delayed. When that happens:

- the amount that wasn't covered stays on your balance, and a default charge is added on it;
- you can see it on your **Dashboard** and on **My Repayments** (`/repayments`);
- you can pay the shortfall yourself with **Liquidate Loan** on the Repayments page, so it doesn't grow;
- if you believe the deduction was taken but not recorded, pass the conversation to the team with the month, and they'll
  check it against payroll's record.

## Paying off early

On **My Repayments** (`/repayments`), choose **Liquidate Loan**. You'll see how much clears your loan today. Pay that
amount (or part of it) to MicroBuilt's account, then upload the proof of payment. The team checks it, usually within a
working day, and applies it to your loan. You can follow the request's status on the same page.

## Downloading a statement

Open **Statement** (`/statement`) to see every charge and payment on your loan. Choose **Export statement** for a PDF or a
spreadsheet. The file is prepared in the background and arrives in your notifications (and by email, if you have one)
with a download link.

## Signing in and two-factor authentication

- **Forgot your password?** Choose **Forgot password** on the sign-in page. You'll get a link by email, or a code by SMS
  if you signed up with your phone.
- **Other ways to sign in:** an emailed code, a magic link, or a passkey (Settings → Security → Passkeys).
- **Two-factor authentication:** turn it on in **Settings → Security** with an authenticator app. Keep your backup codes
  somewhere safe: they get you in if you lose your phone.
- **Locked out or lost your 2FA device?** Use a backup code. If you have none, pass the conversation to the team: they
  can reset your sign-in after confirming it's you.
